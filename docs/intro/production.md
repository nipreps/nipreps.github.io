# Scientific production

This page indexes the scientific output of the *NiPreps* community since 2016:
peer-reviewed papers and preprints, conference communications, the community's
presence at the annual meeting of the Organization for Human Brain Mapping (OHBM),
talks and educational events, and the releases of its software.
To cite a *NiPreps* tool in your work, follow the citation boilerplate that each
tool writes with its outputs (see [Transparency of workflows](transparency.md)).

!!! tip "Is something missing?"

    This index is maintained by hand.
    If a paper, abstract, talk or event about *NiPreps* is missing, please
    [edit this page](https://github.com/nipreps/nipreps.github.io/edit/mkdocs/docs/intro/production.md)
    and send a pull request.

## *NiPreps* at OHBM, 2023 to 2026

The OHBM annual meeting is where the *NiPreps* community meets every year: at the
OHBM BrainHack before the meeting, in educational courses and symposia, and in the
poster halls.

- **BrainHack:** the *NiPreps* TrainTrack, June 11 ([slides](https://www.nipreps.org/presentations/20260611-BrainHack/)), and "BIDS @ BrainHack 2026" ([slides](https://www.nipreps.org/presentations/2026-OHBM-BrainHack-BIDS/)).

### Milestone releases

- *fMRIPrep* 1.0.0 (December 7, 2017): the first stable release.
- *fMRIPrep* 20.2.0 (September 28, 2020): the first long-term support (LTS) series.
- *NiReports* 23.0.0 (March 10, 2023): visual reports move into their own library.
- *fMRIPrep* 23.2.0 (January 10, 2024): the *fit & transform* refactor, with the `--level` and `--derivatives` options to reuse precomputed results.
- *MRIQC* 24.0.0 (April 17, 2024): quality control of diffusion MRI.
- *PETPrep* 0.0.2 (September 16, 2025): the first release.
- *fMRIPrep* 25.2.0 (October 2, 2025): the second long-term support series.
- *TemplateFlow* client 25.1.0 (October 21, 2025): a new client interface.
- *NiFreeze* 0.1.0 (February 6, 2026): the first release.
- *MRIQC* 26.0.0 (November 5, 2026): the first release of the 26.x series.
